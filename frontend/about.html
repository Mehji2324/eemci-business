About EEMCI Meknès

Training the leaders of tomorrow through academic excellence and international recognition since 2011.

Our Mission

To provide students with the keys to a successful international career by training leaders in Management, Commerce, and IT. We focus on pedagogical excellence, global recognition of diplomas, and concrete support for professional integration.

Our Vision 2030

EEMCI aims to become the reference school in Africa for its core disciplines, shaping a new generation of intercultural leaders and innovators capable of influencing the global economy.

Core Values

Accredited Excellence

Programs recognized by the Moroccan State and validated internationally.

Global Openness

Providing a "passport to Europe" through a global professional and academic network.

Proximity & Expertise

Personalized coaching and support from an active and committed faculty.

Diversity & Sharing

Focus on intercultural richness and celebrating diverse backgrounds.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our history

2011

Foundation

Established in Meknès to meet market demands for high-level professionals in Management and IT.

2013-15

State Recognition

Obtained official accreditation from the Moroccan State, solidifying the credibility of its diplomas.

2015-16

International

Strategic partnerships with European institutions (FEDE, Miib's) for double-validated degrees.

2018

Alliances

Strengthened ties with global industry leaders like Adecco, Dekra, and Aptiv.
